# Packaging section asset

Final website asset: `public/images/packaging-still-life.webp`.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, with a transparent background. The generated PNG was inspected and converted to a 680 px wide WebP with alpha preserved for website loading. Original generation remains in the Codex generated image library. The asset is newly generated to match the visual direction of the supplied reference, not extracted from that screenshot.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a premium photorealistic 3D product still-life cutout for the lower-left corner of a white packaging sustainability website section. Tall clear PET water bottle with bright orange screw cap at the back left, a small unbranded amber glass bottle beside it, a kraft cardboard packaging box with a simple white recycling symbol in front, and a polished silver aluminium can on the right. Lush realistic fresh green leaves frame the bottles from behind and extend to the right near the base. Tight upright composition with all objects completely visible, clean realistic materials, subtle grounded soft shadow, elegant warm daylight. No words, no lettering, no brand logos, no background, no UI, no diagram. Genuine transparent alpha background. Intended display approximately 240px wide by 320px tall; large detailed render.
